## 2. Supply & demand as if-then rules (GAP = not specified)

1. **Formations.** Demand = DBR or RBR; supply = RBD or DBD. Base = small-body candles; leg-out = large body. GAP: no body/range % and no maximum number of base candles ("two or three, it doesn't matter" (13); 4-5 accepted on weekly/monthly (09, 15)).
2. **Zone versions.** Preferred = "drawn as small as the structure allows"; wider = "wick to wick" (30). GAP: exact construction of the preferred version.
3. **Originality.** IF DBR or RBD, it is "a reaction of a previous level": find the origin (RBR/DBD) to the left, and the stop must cover it (05, 09, 24).
4. **Freshness.** A zone becomes tested once price touches the preferred version (21, 28). "By the third or fourth touch, there's almost nothing left" (13).
5. **Profit margin (mandatory).** The move away from the zone, measured at its extreme in units of preferred-zone height, must be ≥3R; ≥5R is "great"; 2.94 fails (10).
6. **Location.** On the higher timeframe (HTF), draw Fibonacci 0/0.33/0.66/1 from the top of the big supply to the bottom of the big demand. Buy only in the lower third, sell only in the upper third, nothing in the middle (13). GAP: whether the thirds include the zones.
7. **Coverage, his "#1 rule".** The lower-timeframe (LTF) zone must sit inside an HTF zone (03, 04, 13, 20, 22).
8. **Trend.** Use the last 6 HTF swings: two higher lows = up, two lower highs = down, otherwise sideways (ZigZag) (13). He prefers with-trend trades but does not require them.
9. **Arrival.** Counter-trend or sideways only: no FRESH opposing LTF zone within 2R on the arrival path (10, 24).
10. **Timeframes.** Monthly = context, weekly = "working" timeframe, daily = entry (17). Daily zones are refined on 4H/8H/12H/16H; stocks and crude use 2-day charts (09, 23).
11. **Level on top of level (LOL).** The proximal level must be high quality and the gap must be ≤ zone size (GAP: min or max of the two zones). Enter at the DISTAL (outer) level, wider version; IF the levels overlap THEN combine them (05, 15, 20, 28).
12. **Entry-model choice.** IF the wider-version entry has ≥2R of room to the next opposing zone, use a limit there. ELSE IF the preferred version does, use that. ELSE no trade (30).
   - Confirmation entries: pin bar (wick ≥2/3 of the range (12), or ≥2x the body (28)); strict engulfing (close beyond the prior candle's full high/low) (21); inside bar (22).
   - Enter beyond the candle extreme, on the same timeframe as the zone or one lower.
13. **Stop.** Distal line + 33% of zone height: "over 4,000 transactions tested ... the best of all the buffers" (30). Confirmation variant: candle extreme + 33% of zone height (21, 22). Never place it inside any zone (28).
14. **Target.** 3R by default (04, 05, 18); 2R for Nasdaq and counter-trend (10, 20); 4R (09, 15); or just before the next HTF opposing zone (17, 19). Minimum 2R (13).
15. **Management.** Move to break-even at +1R, below a new pivot if one formed (13, 18). At +2R lock +1R under the newest demand zone, later +2R (18, 21). Never widen the stop (27).
16. **Risk.** Fixed dollars: $4k (0.4%), $8k from 3 Aug (21), 2x on top ideas (16, 17, 20). Correlated markets = one idea ("Two at most", 29). No news rule anywhere in this batch.

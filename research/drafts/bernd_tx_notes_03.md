## 2. Supply & demand rules (if-then; EXPLICIT unless marked)
- Gate: IF the fundamental bias (COT/Seasonality/Valuation) is neutral or contradictive THEN no trade (27). Do the analysis on futures, then copy the orders to FX/CFD (28, 09).
- Process: (1) location = HTF zone coverage, (2) direction = HTF trend, (3) timing = LTF zone + qualifiers; "all zone qualifiers must be met" (13).
- Timeframe pairs ("income"): monthly = M+W; weekly = W+D; daily = D+4h (08, 24). His swing: M/W/D context, execute on D, never below 4h; also uses 960 min (16 h), 12 h, 6 h, 8 h (16, 19, 28). Ideal coverage chain M ⊃ W ⊃ D (28).
- Big brother/small brother (#1 rule): IF the LTF zone is not inside a same-side HTF zone THEN skip (13, 22, 08, 02). "The big brother wins" (22).
- Departure (#3 in ranking): LTF leg-out = an explosive candle "abnormally bigger" than the prior candles plus "two three candles of follow through"; HTF: decisive candles are enough; stair-stepping = reject (13). GAP: numeric explosive/decisive/indecisive definitions are not in this batch.
- Base: 1-6 indecisive candles, >6 = reject; he prefers 3-4 (16). Speed bump: IF 1 base candle, same colour as the leg, in the middle of a same-colour momentum run THEN reject; a 1-candle base at the origin of a DBR/RBD is OK (16).
- Freshness (#2), measured to the right after the leg-out (19): LTF beginner = wider version never touched; LTF advanced = preferred version never touched; HTF (W/M only) = preferred may be penetrated "approximately 25%"; never trade the 3rd touch; day traders use LTF rules for the HTF too (19). Apr 2025 version: HTF judged on the preferred version (08). Discretionary exception: a shallow touch + "rocket ship" departure can make a zone stronger (19, 02).
- Originality (advanced, optional): DBR/RBD are non-original unless at ATH/ATL; RBR/DBD are original; IF the price area was visited about 4-6 times in a cluster THEN expect a break and skip; "original zones are [not] better" (08, 25).
- Flip zone: a new demand zone formed inside an old supply zone that the leg-out broke = stronger (and vice versa) (25).
- Level on top of level: stacked zones may be combined or the lower one traded; the distal zone can relax the base count (16, 24, 06).
- Candle patterns (hammer/shooting star: wick >= 2x body; engulfing = range engulf incl. wicks, but his example uses a body test) mark HTF pivots/zones; you still wait for the return to the zone (12).
- Trend: HL-sequence = uptrend; ideal 3 HH + 2 HL, long from the 3rd HL demand (11). GAP: pivot definition.
- Entry: limit order at the proximal line ("beginning of the supply zone") of the LTF zone, preferred or wider version; first touch; set and forget (22, 28, 03). Alternatives allowed: shooting-star break, trendline break (28).
- Stop: "beyond the base" / "a little bit above the supply zone"; stop rule "33% of the zone" (16); IF a fresh swing pivot (last week's high) is just beyond THEN put the stop above the pivot (28).
- Targets: minimum 1:2, ideally >= 1:3 (22, 13); choose one: fixed 1:3/1:4; or exit before the next opposing HTF zone; or hold by COT (28). He usually aims for 3-5R (03, 06, 11).
- Management: break-even at +1R (09 day trade; 28 swing, "Exactly"). No trailing rule found.
- Risk: 0.5-2% per trade (23); 2% per idea split 1%+1% across correlated trades (02); day trading implies 1R = 1% (09). News: avoid for intraday (09). GAPS: preferred/wider definitions, buffer reading of "33%", max positions, "profit margin"/"arrival" qualifiers (announced, not yet taught by Jan 2026).
